A college academic project exploring affiliate marketing, its working process, commission models, major platforms, benefits, challenges, and future trends.

## 📚 Project Overview

Affiliate marketing is a performance-based digital marketing strategy in which individuals or businesses promote products or services and earn commissions for qualifying sales or actions generated through their referral links.

### Topics Covered

- Introduction to affiliate marketing
- Key participants and working process
- Types and channels of affiliate marketing
- Revenue and commission models
- Major affiliate marketing platforms
- Real-world examples and applications
- Benefits, challenges, and future trends
- Conclusion
